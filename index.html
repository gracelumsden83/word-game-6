✦ WORD LADDER ✦

Find the hidden word using the scrambled letters below.
Every new word must keep all the letters from the word before it!

🔒 Blue letters came from the previous word and must all be used again!

💡 HOW TO PLAY:

• Click letters to build the hidden word.

• The letters are scrambled every round.

• Blue letters are carried over from the last word.

• You must use every blue letter.

• Three new letters are added each round.

• Only one of those new letters belongs in the next word.
